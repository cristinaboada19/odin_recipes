Lemon Chicken and Asparagus

Description

Tender chicken breast glazed in a bright lemon sauce, paired with fresh roasted asparagus and seasoned with herbs and garlic. A light, flavorful dish with the perfect balance of citrus and savory notes.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 4 teaspoons salt
  • 4 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound asparagus, ends trimmed and cut into 2-inch segments
  • 1 lemon
  • 3/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Place one chicken breast on a cutting board. Cover chicken breast with plastic wrap and pound with a meat mallet until 3/4-inch thick. Repeat with remaining chicken breasts.
  2. Season chicken with 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a very-large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook until golden brown and an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center of a breast reads at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C), 8 to 10 minutes, turning halfway. Remove chicken from the skillet; set aside.
  4. Add asparagus to the skillet. Cook over medium-high, until bright green and crisp tender, stirring occasionally, 2 to 3 minutes. Remove asparagus from the skillet; set aside.
  5. Juice half of the lemon (about 1 1/2 tablespoon juice) and slice the remaining half in wedges.
  6. Add lemon juice, whipping cream, Parmesan cheese, garlic, and Italian seasoning to the skillet
  7. Simmer over medium-high, scraping the bottom of the skillet with a wooden spoon to release any browned bits, 2 minutes. Season with remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon pepper.
  8. Add chicken and asparagus back into the skillet. Simmer until heated through and sauce reaches desired consistency, about 1 minute. Serve with lemon wedges.
